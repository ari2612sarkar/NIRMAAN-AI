# NIRMAAN AI — 3-Minute Demo Script

Total runtime target: ~3:00. Timestamps are guidance, not a hard script.

---

**[0:00–0:20] Hook — the problem**

> "Most waste-management tech asks 'how do we collect garbage better?'
> NIRMAAN AI asks a bigger question: how do we generate less of it,
> collect only what's actually needed, fix vehicles before they break,
> and know exactly where the money's going — all in one system."

Show the landing dashboard. Point at the five KPIs: waste generated,
operating cost, overflow incidents, high-risk bins, potential monthly
saving.

**[0:20–0:45] AI Priorities Today**

> "Every morning, the system looks at the full operation and ranks the
> highest-impact actions — not generic alerts, but costed
> recommendations."

Scroll to "AI Priorities Today." Click into one recommendation card —
point out the Impact Score, expected monthly saving, implementation
cost, payback period, and the specific drivers behind it (e.g.
"32 overflow incidents in the last 30 days").

**[0:45–1:15] Forecast + anomaly detection**

> "Switch zones and you get a trained forecast, not a guess — day-of-week
> and trend patterns, an 80% confidence band, and why the model predicted
> what it predicted."

Switch the zone selector. Point at the forecast chart's dashed
projection and shaded band. Scroll to Waste Anomalies — point at a
flagged spike and read its explanation out loud ("20% above the 14-day
rolling average").

**[1:15–1:45] Route optimization + predictive maintenance**

> "For the same zone, here's the collection route today versus an
> optimized route — real distance and real ₹ cost, not a mockup."

Show the baseline-vs-optimized route comparison bar and numbers.

> "And here's the fleet, ranked by maintenance risk — not a black-box
> probability, but a transparent score with the actual reasons: mileage,
> overdue service, recent emergency repairs."

Point at the top vehicle row in the Predictive Maintenance table and
read its drivers + recommended action.

**[1:45–2:30] The signature moment — the simulator**

> "This is the part that matters most: what happens if we actually act
> on this?"

Open the Waste & Cost Reduction Simulator. Drag "Source segregation
adoption" from 0 to 50%. Narrate the live recalculation: mixed waste
drops, landfill-diverted % rises, total operating cost falls. Then push
"Route optimization efficiency" up too, and point at the combined
projected monthly/annual saving box updating live.

> "Every one of these numbers comes from the same formulas the
> recommendation engine and the dashboard use — nothing here is a
> separate, disconnected mockup."

**[2:30–2:50] AI Copilot**

> "And if you'd rather just ask —"

Type or click a suggested question into the Copilot ("What if
segregation increased to 60%?" or "Which zone should we prioritize?").
Read the grounded answer out loud.

> "No external LLM, no hallucinated numbers — it's calling the exact
> same backend functions you just saw on screen."

**[2:50–3:00] Close**

> "Predict less waste. Collect smarter. Maintain proactively. Spend
> less. That's NIRMAAN AI."

Return to the dashboard KPI row for the final frame.
